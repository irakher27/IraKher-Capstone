# Room — Creativity Without the Scroll

## Idea
A room-based app that fights "scroll instead of create" by removing all decision-making
from starting. At scheduled dead-time slots (after class, before bed) or a manual
"I'm bored" tap, it sends one pre-chosen 5–15 min creative micro-prompt matched to
time/mood/materials on hand — no browsing, no feed. Finishing a prompt adds a visible
item (plant, poster, string lights) to an evolving digital room. A small circle of
close friends can passively see each other's rooms fill up over time — no likes,
comments, or streaks, just ambient visibility.

Core loop: nudge → 1 prompt → timer → finish → room grows → occasionally glance at a friend's room.

## MVP Scope
- Manual "I'm bored" button + basic scheduled reminder (no sensors/location)
- Single-suggestion card matched by time/mood/materials, with limited swaps
- ~100 curated (not AI-generated) micro-prompts
- Digital room that fills with earned items on completion
- Friend circle (~8 max), passive room grid, no likes/comments/streaks

## Final Goal (post-MVP)
- Smart nudge timing based on real usage patterns
- Physical-craft-to-digital-item syncing (real object → matching room item)
- AI-assisted prompt variation from unusual material combos (human-reviewed before publish)
- Expanded social layer (still ambient, never a feed)

## AI-Involvement Level
**Target: AI-assisted curation, not AI-generated content — for MVP.**
Reasoning: the core risk isn't running out of ideas, it's trust — a bad or
generic-feeling suggestion breaks the "removed friction" promise the whole app
is built on. Hand-curated prompts guarantee quality while the core loop
(nudge → room payoff) is validated. AI generation is planned for v2+, and even
then scoped to *variations* of vetted prompts with human review, not raw
unreviewed output to users.
